# Chapter 09 — Project

Version: 0.1
Status: Draft

---

## 1. Purpose

A Project is a **bounded initiative**.

Not all work is ongoing responsibility. Some work has a goal and an end: launch a product, complete a migration, resolve a major incident, deliver a study. A Project is the object that gathers the right Employees, Artifacts, Commitments, and Tasks around such a goal and gives them a shared context.

Where a Workspace is the organization, a Project is an initiative *within* the organization.

---

## 2. Responsibilities

A Project is accountable for:

- **Defining scope** — what the initiative includes and, just as importantly, what it does not.
- **Naming a goal** — the definition of done.
- **Gathering participants** — the Employees (and humans) collaborating on it.
- **Providing shared context** — a single place where the initiative's Artifacts, Commitments, and Tasks come together.

A Project is a frame, not a worker. It organizes work; it does not perform it.

---

## 3. Owns

A Project owns:

- Its **goal and scope statement**.
- Its **participant list** — which Employees and humans are involved (participation, not ownership of the Employees themselves).
- Its **Artifacts** — the outputs produced for the initiative.
- Its **Commitments and Tasks** — the work scoped to the initiative.
- Its **timeline** — milestones, deadlines, and status.

A Project belongs to a Workspace. Its Employees still belong to the Workspace; the Project only records who is participating.

---

## 4. Doesn't Own

A Project does **not** own:

- **The Employees** — they participate; the Workspace owns them.
- **The Employees' other work** — an Employee may serve many Projects and many Commitments.
- **The Brains or Knowledge** — those are shared workspace assets.
- **Execution** — the Project does not do the work; its participant Employees do.

A Project that tries to own its Employees exclusively has become a silo, not a collaboration.

---

## 5. Lifecycle

```
Proposed → Active → On Hold → Completed → Archived
              ▲                  │
              └──────────────────┘   (reopened, if needed)
```

- **Proposed** — defined but not yet started.
- **Active** — work is underway.
- **On Hold** — paused; preserved for resumption.
- **Completed** — the goal is met; the Project may be closed.
- **Archived** — retained for history alongside its Artifacts.

A Project is meant to end. A Project that never completes was probably a Commitment in disguise.

---

## 6. Future Extension

The Project concept may grow to support:

- **Templates** — pre-defined Projects (a standard team, a standard set of Tasks) that can be cloned.
- **Portfolios** — grouping related Projects for oversight.
- **Dependencies** — declaring that one Project's output is another's input.
- **Milestones and reporting** — structured progress visible to humans and Employees alike.
- **Cross-workspace collaboration** — inviting an Employee from another Workspace into a Project under controlled terms.
